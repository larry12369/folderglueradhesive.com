Reliable Adhesive for Pharmaceutical Boxes – QL-118GH

Pharmaceutical packaging requires the highest standards of safety, hygiene, and durability. QL-118GH adhesive for pharmaceutical boxes delivers all three, making it a trusted choice for packaging medicines and healthcare products.

Safe & Strong Bonding
QL-118GH is a water-based adhesive with no harmful chemicals, making it safe for pharmaceutical packaging. It creates a strong, tamper-resistant seal that maintains integrity during transportation and storage.

Product Highlights

  • Clean Application: No stringing or excessive glue marks.

  • Temperature Resistance: Withstands cold chain logistics down to -30°C and heat up to 80°C.

  • Material Compatibility: Works with coated paper, laminated board, and varnished finishes.

  • Eco-Friendly: Meets environmental packaging regulations.

Industry Application Example
A pharmaceutical packaging company in the Middle East switched to QL-118GH to prevent glue contamination and improve sealing strength. The result was a 40% drop in packaging defects.

Conclusion
When safety and performance are critical, QL-118GH is the adhesive for pharmaceutical boxes that manufacturers trust.
