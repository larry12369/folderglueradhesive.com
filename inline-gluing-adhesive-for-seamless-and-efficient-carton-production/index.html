Inline Gluing Adhesive for Seamless and Efficient Carton Production

Efficiency defines success in packaging. Our inline gluing adhesive is developed for manufacturers who want seamless integration with automated lines. It allows cartons to be glued, folded, and sealed in one smooth process, eliminating bottlenecks and reducing downtime.

This adhesive ensures precise application, fast tack, and clean results—making it the ideal choice for folder gluer adhesive systems. As a strong carton sealing adhesive, it guarantees reliable bonding across a wide range of packaging materials.

Its versatility extends to both box sealing adhesive and folding carton adhesive applications, offering durability under transport and heavy use. Plus, being a water-based packaging adhesive, it is eco-friendly, cost-effective, and easy to clean from machinery.

For manufacturers looking to improve speed, reduce waste, and deliver flawless packaging, our inline gluing solution is the answer.
