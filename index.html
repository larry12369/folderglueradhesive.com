Who we are

Hebei Xinbang Packaging Materials Co., Ltd. Established in 2000, We are a high-tech enterprise specializing in the research, development, and manufacturing of eco-friendly adhesives.we have invested over RMB 30 million in product development and collaborative research with top academic institutions.

About Us

What we do

We are a high-tech enterprise specializing in the research, development, and manufacturing of eco-friendly adhesives.We focus on water-based resin, acrylic, and polyurethane systems, offering comprehensive adhesive solutions for the paper packaging and material lamination industries.

Our Work

Where we work

Xinbang is headquartered in Shijiazhuang, the capital city of Hebei Province China. We maintain close industry-university research partnerships with prestigious universities such as Beijing University of Chemical Technology, Beijing Institute of Technology, and South China University of Technology,etc.

WE RECOMMeND WATER-BASED ADHESIVE

High Speed Folder Gluer Adhesive 306P

Designed for high-speed folder gluer machines with roller coating, stable up to 400 meters/min without splashing.

Semi-Automatic Gluing Adhesive 118GH

Suitable for semi-automatic gluing machines with roller coating. Versatile adhesive for corrugated cartons, pharmaceutical boxes, cosmetic boxes.

Manual Application Adhesive 108H

Manual brushing glue with long open time, easy to apply, no stringing, low odor, smooth flow. Ideal for paper bags, corrugated cartons, color boxes.

Automatic Inline Gluing Adhesive 719P

Suitable for high-speed inline gluing of 5-ply, 7-ply, and corrugated board.Strong initial tack, fast running speed, compatible with kraft paper, white card, flexo print, red envelopes, envelopes.

Carton Box Gluing Adhesive

Versatile adhesive for manual, semi-auto, high-speed, and inline carton gluing.for industrial cartons, yellow board cartons, e-commerce boxes.Smooth flow, no splashing, no stringing.

 Glue Making Machine

Fully automatic operation, labor-saving. Can use regular household AC power, no space limitation.Used to produce carton glue, laminating glue, starch glue, kraft paper glue, etc.

Film Lamination Adhesive QL-3800

Designed for medium-speed lamination (approx. 50 m/min), Good leveling and water resistance, Suitable for dairy, fruit, and FMCG packaging.

Hot Melt Adhesive

Strong bonding, high tensile strength, Good thermal stability, low odor, Yellow transparent blocks.
